Sociodemographics & market potential

Basic Sociodemographics - Mexico (AGEB-Areas and Municipios) ↗ Premium

Michael Bauer International (MBI) · 2026 · AGEB areas + municipios · updated annually · also at municipio ↗ and 5-digit postal code ↗

Current-year estimates of resident population (P_T), households and household size (HH_T, HH_SIZE) and population by gender and five age bands (AGE_T0014, AGE_T1529, AGE_T3044, AGE_T4559, AGE_T60PL, with AGE_F… / AGE_M… splits) for every AGEB and municipio in Mexico, refreshed every year so territory sizing does not drift as cities grow. P_PRM expresses each area's share of the national population, a convenient weight for normalising indicators across territories. Because MBI publishes its economic datasets on the same geographies, all of them join on the same geoid with no re-aggregation.

Purchasing Power - Mexico (AGEB-Areas and Municipios) ↗ & Consumer Spending - Mexico (AGEB-Areas and Municipios) ↗ Premium

Michael Bauer International (MBI) · Purchasing Power 2026 · Consumer Spending 2026 · AGEB areas + municipios · updated annually · Consumer Spending: 20 expenditure categories × 4 measures

These are the market-potential indicators the Data Lab is looking for. Purchasing Power gives disposable income per capita (PP_EURO), as a total per area (PP_MIO) and as an index against the national average (PP_CI, country = 100). Consumer Spending breaks household expenditure into 20 categories, each with the same four measures (per capita, total, index and share of country); the ones that matter most for a coatings business are tools and equipment for house and garden (CS10_EURO, CS10_MIO, CS10_CI) and routine household maintenance (CS11_EURO, CS11_MIO, CS11_CI), with furniture and furnishings (CS06_*) and household appliances (CS08_*) as signals of home-improvement activity. Summed per AGEB, they give a category-specific addressable-market estimate — the numerator of any white-space analysis — and a fair basis for balancing sales territories by potential rather than by area.

Points of interest — two options

Points Of Interest - Mexico (Latitude/Longitude) ↗ Premium · Option A

HERE · v1 · point geometry · updated quarterly (annual licence also available) · global reference POI provider

HERE's curated POI database for Mexico, with a consistent global category system (category_id, category_name), chain identification (chain_id, chain_name), full address in original and transliterated form (address_original, postal_code), contact details (contacts) and per-record quality scores for name, address and openness (quality_is_name_correct, quality_is_address_correct, quality_is_open). The best fit when PPG México wants a stable, well-documented reference layer to map hardware and paint retailers, automotive workshops and construction-related businesses, and to keep category definitions consistent with other markets.

Places, monthly - Mexico (Latitude/Longitude) ↗ Premium · Option B

dataPlor · v3 · point geometry · refreshed monthly (quarterly and annual licences also available) · Latin America specialist

A living commercial POI layer built for Latin American markets: brand-resolved (chain_name, chain_id), hierarchically categorised (main_category → sub_category → business_category, plus naics_code), with current operating status (open_status, dataplor_status) and engagement signals (popularity_score, dwell_time). The best fit when PPG México wants to track competitor and partner outlets as they open and close month by month, and to weight commercial presence by how busy places are rather than by counts alone. Every record carries a data_quality_confidence_score for transparent filtering.

Mobility & traffic

Traffic Density - Mexico (Road Segment) ↗ & Speed Profiles - Mexico (Road Segment) ↗ Premium

TomTom · Traffic Density 2024-06 · Speed Profiles 2022-12 · road-segment linestrings · updated semesterly / annually

Vehicle traffic from TomTom's probe network on every road segment in Mexico. Traffic Density gives probe counts per weekday and hour (mon … sun, fri_17, sat_11), i.e. how much passing traffic a store front or distributor site is exposed to and when; Speed Profiles add free-flow and time-slot speeds (SPFREEFLOW, speed, REL_SP) for congestion-aware drive-time catchments. Together they support store-accessibility scoring and realistic delivery-territory design for PPG México's distribution.

Daytime Population - Mexico (AGEB-Areas and Municipios) ↗ Premium

Michael Bauer International (MBI) · 2025 · AGEB areas + municipios · updated annually

A mobility variable on the same geography as the MBI sociodemographics: daytime population (P_D) against resident population (P_T) per AGEB. The ratio flags employment and commercial hubs that resident-only analysis undercounts — typically where professional painters and contractors buy during the working day — and joins to the other MBI layers on geoid.

PPG México's own sell-out by store and distributor, store and partner network, and customer accounts is the highest-value ingredient it already owns: joined to the layers above, it calibrates a market-potential model on real performance and turns "high potential, low coverage" areas into a ranked white-space list per channel.
Pricing: all datasets in this proposal are premium; pricing depends on scope (national vs. selected states or metro areas, number of datasets and licence term) and will be quoted by the CARTO Data Partnerships team once PPG México selects its priority layers.
